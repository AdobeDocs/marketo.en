# CX Enterprise Coworker panel in Marketo Engage (Beta) {#coworker-panel-overview}

The Coworker panel lets you open CX Enterprise Coworker directly from Marketo Engage, so you can ask questions without switching to a separate interface.

This embedded panel is a beta available to selected Marketo Engage customers. It runs alongside the existing CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience rather than replacing it, and the two keep separate conversation history and context. As a beta, the panel may continue to change.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>This article is only for those participating in the Coworker panel closed beta. Please do not disseminate.

## What to expect during the beta {#what-to-expect-during-the-beta}

| Area | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Open the embedded beta from within Marketo Engage. The existing CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience remains available through its current access point. |
| Existing conversations | Your existing conversation history remains available in the current CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience. Enabling the beta does not remove that history. |
| History and context | The beta and the existing experience have separate conversation history and context. A conversation in one is not available in the other. |
| Existing functionality | You can continue to use the functionality available to you in the current experience. |
| Responses and interface | Responses, controls, and layouts may differ between the two experiences. |

## Open and use the Coworker panel {#open-and-use-the-coworker-panel}

1. In Marketo Engage, select the **Coworker** icon in the top navigation.

SCREENSHOT

1. Enter your request. Include the details Coworker needs, such as a program name or date range; don't assume it knows which item you're viewing.

1. Review the response. Ask follow-up questions if you need to clarify or refine it.

1. Close the panel when you want to return to your Marketo Engage workspace.

1. Review generated responses and any proposed changes before using them.

## Conversation history and context {#conversation-history-and-context}

Conversation history and context are not shared between the Coworker panel in Marketo Engage and CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage. Each experience maintains its own conversations. You can't resume a conversation from one experience in the other.

When you switch experiences, start a new conversation and provide the relevant background, requirements, and details again.

## Continue using CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage {#continue-using-cx-enterprise-coworker-for-marketo-engage}

You don't have to move your existing work to try the beta. Open the current CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience through its existing access point when you want to revisit a previous conversation or use functionality available there.

Your existing history and functionality remain available in that experience. They don't appear in the embedded beta, and continued access to the current experience doesn't mean the two experiences have feature parity.

## Differences between the experiences {#differences-between-the-experiences}

The embedded beta and CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage are separate experiences. You may notice differences even when you enter the same prompt.

- **Responses:** Wording, organization, level of detail, or presentation may vary.
- **Interface:** Chat controls, layouts, and how you review responses may differ.

Don't expect identical responses or an identical interface. If a capability you use today isn't available in the beta, continue using it in the current experience.

## Frequently asked questions {#frequently-asked-questions}

### Does the beta replace CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage?

No. Both experiences are available in parallel during the beta.

### Will I lose my existing conversations or functionality?

No. Enabling the beta doesn't remove your existing conversation history or functionality from the current CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience.

### Is conversation history or context shared between the two experiences?

No. Each experience has separate conversation history and context. To revisit a conversation, open the experience where you started it. When switching, provide the background and details needed for your request again.

### Why can the same prompt produce a different response?

The experiences are separate, so their responses may differ in wording, organization, level of detail, or presentation. Review the substance of a response before using it.

### What should I do if I can't see the beta?

Ask your Marketo Engage administrator whether your subscription is participating in the beta.

### What if I encounter a problem with the beta?

You can continue using the current CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage experience. Ask your Marketo Engage administrator for support or feedback instructions for the beta.
