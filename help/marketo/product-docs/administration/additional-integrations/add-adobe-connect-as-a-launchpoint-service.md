# Add [!DNL Adobe Connect] as a [!DNL LaunchPoint] Service {#add-adobe-connect-as-a-launchpoint-service}

Marketo manages your [!DNL Adobe Connect] webinar registration and attendance.

>[!PREREQUISITES]
>
>* An existing subscription to [!DNL Adobe Connect] and administration rights are required. Have the following details on hand: username, password, participant password, and meeting folder URL.

>[!NOTE]
>
>* **Admin Permissions Required**
>* [!DNL Adobe Connect On Site] is not currently supported.

1. In your My Marketo, click **[!UICONTROL Admin]**.

1. Under **[!UICONTROL Integration]**, select **[!UICONTROL LaunchPoint]**.

   ![](assets/add-adobe-connect-as-a-launchpoint-service-1.png)

1. Click **[!UICONTROL New]** and select **[!UICONTROL New Service]**.

   ![](assets/add-adobe-connect-as-a-launchpoint-service-2.png)

1. Enter a **[!UICONTROL Display Name]**. In the **[!UICONTROL Service]** drop-down, select **[!UICONTROL Adobe Connect]**.

   ![](assets/add-adobe-connect-as-a-launchpoint-service-3.png)

1. Enter your **[!UICONTROL Username]**, **[!UICONTROL Access Code]**, **[!UICONTROL Meeting Folder URL]**, and **[!UICONTROL Participant Password]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Create]**.

   ![](assets/add-adobe-connect-as-a-launchpoint-service-4.png)

   >[!TIP]
   >
   >When you create the participant password, the value you choose is included in a query string when the links are sent out for the event. It is suggested that you make it customer-friendly.

   >[!NOTE]
   >
   >After you log in to your [!DNL Adobe Connect] account, select the **[!UICONTROL Seminars]** tab. Without drilling down into any specific seminar, copy the URL from your browser bar into the **[!UICONTROL Meeting Folder URL]** setting.

Your [!DNL Adobe Connect] service is now synced with Marketo Engage.
